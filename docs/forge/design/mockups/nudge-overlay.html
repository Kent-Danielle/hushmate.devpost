Nudge overlay and notifications

What the recipient sees: a system notification (if they turned notifications on), then, when they open or refocus Hushmate, one overlay that must be acknowledged with Got it. Without notifications the overlay still appears on the next open. The sender is never named anywhere; the words come from the app.

Static mockup, 390px phone frames, dark and light side by side. Values come from tokens.css (a copy of the design system's). Frames are labelled with the spec.md and prd.md headings they implement.

The overlay

Overlay: default

Scrim fades in, then the card rises 8px (--hm-rise, slow ease-out). App icon, the message in the Voice style (Fredoka 500 18), and a full-width mint Got it. There is no close button and the scrim does not dismiss: it must be acknowledged. It sits above everything, including the tab bar.

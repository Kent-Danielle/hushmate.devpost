Hushmate

Less shush, more hush.

A hosted, mobile-first PWA where housemates, students in shared housing first, share their quiet times and can nudge each other anonymously, with the app itself as the messenger.

The journey, drawn

The core journey

  1. Open the link. The landing page offers Sign up or Sign in. There's nothing to install.
  2. Sign up with name, email and password.
  3. Onboarding: create a house or join one, by invite link or by searching an ID like MapleHouse#482913.
  4. A join request waits for the owner to accept it. Creating a house needs no approval.
  5. Optionally set your first quiet times (skippable).
  6. Your first house becomes your active house.
  7. Turn on notifications. On iPhone, add Hushmate to the Home Screen first.
  8. Land on Schedule: today's calendar with each housemate's quiet times side by side, and Nudge.
  9. Day to day: check who's resting now and post heads-ups on Summary.
  10. Someone's loud → Nudge → the app reminds them in its own voice. They never learn it was you.
  11. They tap Got it. You quietly see it was acknowledged.
  12. Every nudge-free day grows the house's Quiet Streak.
  1. Open the link and sign in with one of three demo accounts (credentials are on the Devpost page and in the README).
  2. A welcome card explains DevPost Demo House: three simulated housemates, and nudges that arrive often.
  3. Turn on notifications on this device.
  4. See the house on Schedule. One simulated housemate has a morning quiet time.
  5. Nudge a housemate: a bot, or another judge on a different demo account.
  6. Within 10 minutes, a Demo nudge arrives as a real notification.
  7. Open it and tap Got it.
  8. More Demo nudges follow every 30 minutes to 2 hours until you log out.
Success: a gentle, anonymous nudge arrives as a real notification on someone's own device, they acknowledge it, and the sender quietly learns it landed. Nobody else in the house knows.

Landing & onboarding sign up / in → house → quiet times → notifications

On screen & behaviour

  • First house → active automatically. Houses joined later start inactive assume
  • Waiting for approval → notification setup is offered right after the request, so you hear when you're approved assume
  • Housemates only ever see your name

Edge states

  • Waiting for approval: Houses shows Pending, and Schedule shows an empty state inviting you to create a house or wait assume
  • Notifications declined: everything still works

How we'll know

  • Sign-up to Schedule of a house you created, without leaving the flow
  • Skipping quiet times still completes onboarding
  • The notification step shows iPhone Home Screen guidance
  • No screen shows a housemate's email
Schedule home: day view, week toggle, Nudge

On screen & behaviour

  • Day view by default, with a week toggle and no month view
  • One lane per person, so overlaps sit side by side. “You” are lavender, and each housemate gets a pastel
  • Filled = counts in this house now. Outlined + “away” = another house is active
  • A “now” line. The Nudge button floats bottom-right design: confirm
  • Quiet-time editor: start and end time pickers in 15-minute steps, plus days Monday to Sunday = one set
  • End earlier than start → “11:00 PM → 7:00 AM (next day)”, belonging to the night it starts suggest
  • Editing a set asks “just this day, or all days?” Just this day splits it off assume
  • Add, edit and delete only in your active house, plus the optional step when joining

Edge states

  • No quiet times yet → “No quiet times yet” → Add quiet time
  • Inactive house: viewable, not editable

How we'll know

  • Three overlapping quiet times all show side by side
  • “11:00 PM → 7:00 AM, Mon/Wed/Fri” continues into the next morning
  • “Just this day” vs “all days” edits behave as described
  • Only 15-minute steps can be picked
  • Switching day ↔ week keeps the same house and date
Nudge picker & confirmation the kernel, sender side

On screen & behaviour

  • Nudge at any time, not just during your own quiet time
  • Toast: “Nudge sent quietly” design: confirm

Nudge overlay & “Got it” the kernel, recipient side

On screen & behaviour

  • A notification if it's on. Tapping it, or just opening the app, shows the overlay
  • The app icon, a random message in the app's voice, and a full-width mint Got it
  • Must be acknowledged to dismiss. It appears on the next open even without notifications
  • Several waiting → one overlay, and Got it clears them all assume
  • The sender learns quietly: a Summary card only they see, kept 24 hours 24 hours, plus a toast if they open the app within 1 hour. No notification

How we'll know

  • Opening after a nudge shows the overlay before anything else
  • The message never contains the sender's name
  • Across many nudges, more than one of the five messages appears
  • The sender sees “acknowledged”, and no other housemate sees anything
  • Opening within an hour of the acknowledgement shows the toast
Summary the house message board

On screen & behaviour

  • Quiet Streak: days since anyone in the house was nudged, counting up. Nobody knows who broke it
  • Resting now: housemates currently in quiet time, with the breathing ring
  • Heads-ups: free text (200 characters max) with a time and the author's name. No notification
  • A heads-up disappears once its time has passed assume
  • Your acknowledged nudges (only you see them)

Edge states

  • Nobody resting: said warmly, not left blank
  • No heads-ups: an invitation to post one

How we'll know

  • More than 200 characters can't be posted, and a character count is shown
  • A heads-up shows the author and time to everyone in that house
  • After a nudge, the streak shows 0 without naming anyone
  • Someone mid-quiet-time appears under Resting now

DevPost Demo House for judges

On screen & behaviour

  • 6 members: 3 bots that feel like real people (names, weekly quiet times including a morning one, heads-ups) and 3 demo accounts
  • Demo accounts are locked: no changes to name, email or password; no deleting; no leaving, creating, joining or switching houses
  • People sharing an account share its nudges and edits (accepted)
  • A nightly reset returns it to the starting state, with fresh bot heads-ups each day fresh heads-ups
  • Bot nudges: the first within 10 minutes of signing in, then every 30 minutes to 2 hours, never stopping, each labelled “Demo”. Signing out stops them on that device assume
  • Welcome card (option A) copy: “Welcome to DevPost Demo House. [bot names] are simulated housemates. They'll nudge you every 30 minutes to 2 hours so you can feel how Hushmate works. Log out anytime to stop the nudges.”
  • Streak fixed at 10 days note copy: “This is a demo house, so its Quiet Streak is fixed at 10 days. In your own house, it counts the days since anyone was nudged.”
  • No special demo UI: judges use the normal sign-in

How we'll know

  • Each demo account lands in the demo house and sees the welcome card
  • A Demo nudge arrives within 10 minutes, and the next within 30 minutes to 2 hours
  • No locked control is reachable from a demo account
  • After the reset, yesterday's judge edits are gone, and the bots' schedules and heads-ups are back
  • The streak always shows 10 days, with its note

Decisions and boundary

Your product decisions 21
  • Mobile-first PWA, not native: no offline mode and no install prompts, just what notifications need. Native is a future iteration
  • Anonymous nudging is the kernel, and the app is the messenger
  • Name, email and password sign-up, and housemates see only names. Look into a third-party sign-in provider in 4-spec
  • Join requests need the owner's approval
  • One active house: “You don't want to be randomly pinged by housemate A when you're on vacation”
  • Away quiet times are outlined, not filled
  • Quiet times are per house and optional, so people can join just to be mindful
  • Edit only in your active house, except during the join step
  • Day and week views only. Month view deferred
  • No “quiet time starting” reminders. Someone napping “might get startled”
  • Only two notifications: nudged, and join approved
  • Confirm before nudging someone in their quiet time, but allow it
  • Nudge at any time
  • Five rotating messages, picked at random (“Duolingo vibes”)
  • A 5-minute nudge limit against spam
  • The sender learns of the “got it” quietly, never by notification
  • Only the sender and the recipient know about a nudge
  • The Quiet Streak counts nudge-free days. Tradeoff accepted
  • Heads-ups: 200 characters, a time, the author's name, no notification
  • Demo: 3 locked accounts + 3 bots, reset nightly, instead of a public join link
  • No special demo UI: “it will look like a school project.” The demo streak is fixed at 10 days
Deferred from the PoC 5
  • Month viewWeekly quiet times repeat. You deferred it.
  • Owner powers beyond accept/decline (remove members, transfer ownership, delete a house) assume
  • Leaving a house assumeNot discussed.
  • Password reset and account editing for normal accounts assumeThese may come free with a sign-in provider (4-spec).
  • Time zones across houses assumeSame local time assumed for the PoC.
Later 3
  • One-off quiet times announced ahead (interviews, calls)
  • Email notifications as a fallback
  • A native phone app for fuller notification support
Non-goals 6
  • Noise detection (microphone, meters, hardware)The person trying to rest decides.
  • “Quiet time is starting” remindersAnnoying, and they could startle someone napping.
  • AI-proposed quiet hours and AI-reworded nudgesAgent suggestions you didn't take up.
  • Revealing senders, or showing nudge history to the houseThis would break the kernel.
  • A special demo login UIYou rejected it.
  • Penalties or leaderboardsPositive reinforcement, not enforcement.
